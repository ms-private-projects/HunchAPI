# Hunch hackathon film

`hunch-hackathon.mp4` is the three-minute cut made for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon
judges (1920×1080) and `poster.jpg` its still. It keeps the marketing film's opening, funding,
daily-review and sharing scenes ([`../hunch-demo-video`](../hunch-demo-video)) and replaces the
middle with real screens and a chapter on how Hunch uses Muse:

- **Critique, research and build are recordings of the Hunch site** on the local stack, for the
  take "AI needs power" created for the film: the thesis critique arriving from Muse Spark 1.3
  (27.7 s), research 1 by Muse Wonder 1.0 (42.3 s: 6 claims, 8 candidates with roles, 65 cited
  evidence items) and the basket the guardrails built from it (NextEra and Vistra capped at the
  15% limit, 10% cash). Daily prices on that stack are synthetic, and the end card says so.
- **"Under the hood"** names the four jobs and the two models, the Meta Model API, the schema
  bounds, the recorded request and the test that pins every deployed model call to Muse.
- The fund, daily-review, share and Muse-app scenes are styled cards with illustrative data, as in
  the marketing cut; "Hunch inside the Muse app" is labelled coming soon.

The film was made as a [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) project (HTML and CSS
animated with GSAP). The project, its captures and media stay on the team's machines; only this cut
is committed. The voice is a Fish Audio Drama 3 voice invented for the film (no real person
cloned); the music was generated with ACE-Step 1.5. The Alpaca, Interactive Brokers, Meta and Muse
logos belong to their owners, and no partnership is implied.
