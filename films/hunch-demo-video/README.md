# Hunch demo film

`hunch-demo.mp4` is the three-minute product film (1920×1080, 10 MB) and `poster.jpg` its still.
It follows a person from a hunch to a portfolio: writing a take, pressure-testing it with the Hunch
Agent, research, the build under their rules, practice, funding at Alpaca or Interactive Brokers,
the daily review and sharing, and it ends on Hunch inside Muse, which is coming soon.

The film was made during the hackathon as a [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) project:
every screen is HTML and CSS animated with GSAP and rendered by the `hyperframes@0.9.5` CLI. To
keep this repository light, the project, its media and the full-quality render stay on the team's
machines; only this cut is committed.

- The basket, returns, fork counts and leaderboard are illustrative, and the end card says so.
  Practice returns are labelled simulated.
- The music was generated with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT). The voice-over was generated with Fish Audio
  Drama 3; its outputs are ours under the Fish Audio Community License.
- The Alpaca, Interactive Brokers, Meta and Muse logos belong to their owners, and no partnership
  is implied. The avatar on the Muse screen is our own drawing.
